Our philosophy

Pi never rounds. It holds for every circle ever drawn and will hold for every one still to come. We hold our accounting to the same standard: exact today, and just as sound decades from now.

01

Precision

Every figure is reconciled, every assumption written down. Complex wealth and large enterprises deserve accounts that stand up to any audit, regulator or buyer.

02

Discretion

Your affairs are known only to the partner and team assigned to you. We do not publish client names, and we never discuss one client's business with another.

03

Permanence

We plan for the next generation, not the next quarter. Many of our engagements begin with one founder and continue with their children and their companies.

Services

Accounting, tax and CPA services

Three practices, one standard. Choose the practice that fits your situation, or speak with a partner if your affairs span all three.

For high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth individuals, families and family offices.

  • 01

    Personal tax planning and compliance

    Year-round tax planning for income, capital gains, investment portfolios and property, with every return prepared, reviewed by a partner and filed on time across each jurisdiction where you have obligations.

  • 02

    Wealth structuring and asset protection

    Holding companies, trusts and investment vehicles designed for tax efficiency and long-term protection, with clear accounting for each entity so you always know what you own and where.

  • 03

    Estate, trust and succession planning

    Tax modelling for passing wealth and family businesses to the next generation, coordinated with your legal counsel on wills, trusts and shareholder agreements.

  • 04

    Family office accounting

    Consolidated reporting across entities, bank accounts and asset classes, bill payment, staff payroll and investment performance reporting for single-family and multi-family offices.

  • 05

    Cross-border and expatriate tax

    Residency and domicile planning, double tax treaty relief and multi-country compliance for globally mobile individuals, returning residents and families with assets in several countries.

  • 06

    Philanthropy and charitable giving

    Setting up and administering foundations, donor-advised funds and charitable trusts, with the accounting and filings that keep them compliant and tax-efficient.

For large private companies, listed groups and their boards.

  • 01

    Audit and assurance

    Statutory audits, special-purpose audits and assurance engagements carried out with partner involvement throughout, giving boards, lenders and investors confidence in the numbers.

  • 02

    Corporate tax strategy and compliance

    Group tax structuring, transfer pricing, tax provisioning and corporate returns, planned around your commercial goals rather than added after the fact.

  • 03

    Outsourced CFO and controllership

    Senior finance leadership on demand: board reporting, budgeting and forecasting, cash and treasury management, and preparation for audits and financing.

  • 04

    Transaction advisory and due diligence

    Financial and tax due diligence for mergers, acquisitions, disposals and capital raises, plus quality-of-earnings reports and post-deal integration of finance functions.

  • 05

    Financial reporting under IFRS and US GAAP

    Preparation and conversion of financial statements under IFRS and US GAAP, technical accounting opinions and consolidation for multi-entity groups.

  • 06

    Internal controls, risk and governance

    Design and testing of internal controls, fraud risk reviews and governance frameworks that satisfy auditors, regulators and boards.

US CPA services for individuals and businesses with US tax obligations, at home or abroad.

  • 01

    US individual and business tax returns

    Preparation and filing of Form 1040, 1040-NR, 1120, 1120-S, 1065 and related state returns for US citizens, green card holders, non-residents and US-connected businesses.

  • 02

    IRS representation and tax resolution

    Representation before the IRS for audits, notices, penalty abatement, payment plans and voluntary disclosure, handled directly by a licensed CPA.

  • 03

    FBAR, FATCA and international reporting

    FinCEN 114 (FBAR), Form 8938, 5471, 8865 and 3520 filings, and streamlined compliance procedures for Americans living abroad who are behind on their filings.

  • 04

    Compilation, review and attestation

    CPA-issued compilation, review and agreed-upon-procedures reports for lenders, investors, regulators and US counterparties.

  • 05

    US entity formation and tax registration

    Choosing between an LLC, C corporation or S corporation, obtaining an EIN and ITIN, and setting up the books correctly from the first day of trading in the United States.

  • 06

    Multi-state and sales tax compliance

    Nexus reviews, state income and franchise tax filings, and sales tax registration and returns for businesses selling into several US states.

Approach

  2. II

Questions

Questions clients ask

If your question is not here, a partner will answer it in a first consultation.

What does Pi do?

Pi is a private accounting and CPA firm. We provide tax planning, audit and assurance, wealth structuring, estate and succession planning, outsourced CFO services and US CPA services to high-net-worth individuals, family offices and large enterprises.

Who are Pi's typical clients?

Our clients are high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth individuals, multi-generational families and their family offices, founders and executives, and large private and listed enterprises with complex or cross-border affairs.

Do you offer US CPA services to clients outside the United States?

Yes. Our CPA services include US individual and business tax returns, FBAR and FATCA reporting, IRS representation, and compilation, review and attestation engagements for US persons and businesses living or operating abroad.

How do you help with estate and succession planning?

We model the tax effect of transferring wealth across generations, coordinate with your legal counsel on wills, trusts and holding structures, and prepare the accounting and reporting each structure needs so the plan works as intended when it is used.

Can Pi act as an outsourced CFO for a large company?

Yes. We provide outsourced CFO and controllership services, including board reporting, cash and treasury management, budgeting and forecasting, and preparation for audits, financing rounds and transactions.

How is my information kept confidential?

Every engagement is covered by a written confidentiality agreement. Access to client files is limited to the partner and team assigned to you, and documents are exchanged through an encrypted client portal rather than email.
